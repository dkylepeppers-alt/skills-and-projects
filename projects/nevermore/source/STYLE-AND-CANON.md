# Nevermore v2 authoring brief

## Scope
Preserve the supplied setting and all 67 existing location IDs. Repair crypt progression, make ordinary bedroom circulation use corridors, add the approved twelve woodland locations, and maintain a deliberate first-50 global index. No academy archive, infirmary, or service-room expansion is included.

## Source authority
The uploaded 67-location map is the base. The recovered Addams Family & Nevermore Academy lorebook matches all 26 attached source IDs. The original map and lore export are retained here. Six lore entries receive localized spatial corrections. Other recovered content is preserved rather than expanded. Native portable export includes only the 26 linked source entries, plus four newly authored directories.

## Inventions and boundaries
Ridge Woods, Blackwater Hollow, Western Old Woods and their nine destinations are approved fictional additions, not claims about screen canon or actual Vermont geography. Distances, water depths, ownership histories, and supernatural explanations remain unspecified. Ravencroft stays on academy land. No woodland location establishes a new crypt entrance.

## Visual continuity
Cinematic photorealism, late-autumn mixed Vermont woodland, slate-grey skies, subdued evergreen and russet palette, wet stone and wood, restrained fog. Natural scale and empty reusable environments. Each plate has a distinct landmark and camera; no baked-in cast, text, pins or labels. Original academy/indoor images retain their supplied bytes and bindings.

## Image roles
Every new location receives a landscape reference plate. Woodland sectors use list presentation for their three children, so no sector-map backgrounds are required. The parent Jericho Woodlands retains map presentation and receives an updated overview; its five child markers are aligned by visual inspection. The overview is illustrative, not a surveyed plan. The original background is preserved in original.world-map.json and as a separate source image.

## Access policy
The crypt is one physical complex represented by four disconnected containment components until its links are revealed/opened. The ordinary arrival gate is not a progression lock. Default entrances are hidden and progression doors blocked. There are no automatically executed unlock conditions, boons or state machines. The map owner edits the six canonical links explicitly; the current/location history is not reset by this build.

## Scaling
source/global-index.json is an authoring-only ordered ID list. Its fifty entries are emitted first; all remaining records retain their regional source order. It is not a native map field. New low-level locations should be appended within their regional detail section. Promotion into the global index displaces an index entry but never deletes its location. Regional directories are attached to exact current nodes, not presumed inherited.
